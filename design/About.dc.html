About 關於我們 · since 1983

Washington DC Taiwanese School · Hoahuo Taioaan Haghau · Hua-fu Taiwan Hsueh-hsiao

In 1983, a group of Taiwanese American families in the Washington area founded the Washington DC Taiwanese Language School — later renamed WDCTS — to pass Taiwanese languages, heritage and ways of life to the next generation, and to invite our American neighbors to experience them too.

The first programs covered literature, martial arts and recreation 文、武、康樂. Today we teach students from 18 months to adults every Sunday afternoon in Potomac, MD, with fall and spring semesters.

Watch our introduction video → WDCTS on Wikipedia →
台灣的語言與文化 Taiwanese and Mandarin taught side by side, in a warm setting where children absorb the culture naturally.
家長、老師與學校的信任 A long tradition of close, trusting relationships between parents, teachers and staff — many of them volunteers.
在社區裡表演、慶祝 Festivals, folk art and puppet shows — and our students perform at Taiwanese American events across the DC area.

We don't do this alone

Partners 合作夥伴
OUR SPONSOR · EST. 1982

Taiwanese Youth Arts Foundation (TYAF)
台灣人青少年才藝基金會

The nonprofit that operates WDCTS. TYAF gives young Taiwanese Americans the chance to learn about and share Taiwanese culture — sponsoring the Outstanding Taiwanese American Youth Awards, the school's Lunar New Year party, Heritage Week, Mother's Day and Winter Solstice events, puppet shows, and community causes such as standing up against AAPI hate and disaster relief.
