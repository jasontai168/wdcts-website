EARLY CHILDHOOD · 18–36 MONTHS

幼兒親子律動班

Parent-Child Rhythmical Movement in Taiwanese and Mandarin

Toddlers learn with a parent beside them, through songs, movement and play — in both languages.

  • Numbers 1–10, colors, shapes, body parts 數數、顏色、形狀、五官
  • Opposites: slow / fast / stop, high / low 快慢停、大小
  • Running, walking, hopping, turning 走跑跳轉圈
  • Around 12 everyday Taiwanese phrases
TAIWANESE TRACK · PRE-K – GRADE 12

台語班

Preserving heritage through song, story and writing. Students learn to speak — and to read and write Taiwanese in Modern Taiwanese Language 台語現代文書法.

Textbooks: King-An Taiwanese Reader 金安台語讀本 · Children's Taiwanese Book 兒童台語課本

Age 3 · Age 4 · Kindergarten 兒童台語 3歲班 · 4歲班 · 幼稚園班 Nursery rhymes 唸謠, children's songs, stories, crafts, basic conversation and first words — and getting used to learning in a group.
Grades 1 – 12 台語 一年級 至 十二年級班 Songs, stories, crafts and conversation, plus reading and writing in Modern Taiwanese Language.

MANDARIN TRACK · PRE-K – GRADE 8

華語班

Traditional characters, built on Zhuyin 注音 — the phonetic system used in Taiwan — with radicals, strokes and stroke order.

Textbooks: 學華語開步走 · 學華語向前走 (Let's Learn Chinese)

Age 3 · Age 4 · Kindergarten 兒童華語 3歲班 · 4歲班 · 幼稚園班 Basic conversation, an introduction to Zhuyin, simple words, and learning together as a group.
Grades 1 – 8 華語 一年級 至 八年級班 Reading and writing Zhuyin, radicals 部首, strokes 筆劃 and stroke order 筆順.
High school AP Chinese Interpersonal, interpretive and presentational communication, with mock exams, practice and cultural projects to get ready for the AP exam.

ADULTS · TAIWAN CENTER FOR MANDARIN LEARNING

Adult Mandarin (TCML)

Professional Mandarin for adults in the DC area, sponsored by Taiwan's Overseas Community Affairs Council. In person on Sundays or online on weeknights.
