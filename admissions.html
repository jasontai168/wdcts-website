Admissions 入學報名

All ages from 18 months, all fluency levels — no Taiwanese or Mandarin at home required. 不會講也沒關係，歡迎一起來學。

Spring semester 春季班 Starts Jan 24, 2027 Registration deadline: [PLACEHOLDER]

How to enroll in 4 steps

  1. 1 Choose a program Taiwanese track, Mandarin track, or both. Use the guide below to find the right class.
  2. 2 Register online One short form for youth, one for adults. About 10 minutes.
  3. 3 Pay tuition Check, online bill pay, Zelle, or card / Apple Pay / Google Pay via Square. Reply to your registration email with proof of payment.
  4. 4 See you Sunday 1:30pm at Cabin John Middle School. You'll get your classroom by email.

FAQ 常見問題

We don't speak Taiwanese or Mandarin at home. Can we join?

Yes. WDCTS welcomes students of all fluency levels, including complete beginners and non-Taiwanese families.

Can parents take a class while kids are in school?

Yes — adult Mandarin (TCML) meets in person Sundays 1:30–3:00pm in the same building, and adult enrichment classes run the same afternoon.
