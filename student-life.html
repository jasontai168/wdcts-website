DRAGON BOAT 龍舟隊 · SINCE 2007

Humpbacks & Belugas

Two whale-themed crews race every year at the DC Dragon Boat Festival on the Potomac: the adult Humpbacks bring strength and experience, the youth Belugas bring the energy.
